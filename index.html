The studio

Welcome to HBG

Hi there, thanks for dropping by! We’re Honey Bun Games, an independent video game studio based out of Madrid, Spain.

We started out as a game jam team. Love at First Bite was our entry to the Indie Spain Jam 2023, and the idea had teeth: we took it through the PowerUp+ pre-incubation program and founded the studio to build the full game.

Love at First Bite

You live the life of a vampire surviving through a dating app. Swipe right on tasty humans, swipe left when a profile feels off, because some vampires are better at hiding than others. Matches are limited each day, and your thirst only grows while you decide on tonight’s dinner.

  • Profiles are puzzles. Read bios, photos and chats to unmask the vampires in disguise.
  • Thirst is a clock. Limited matches and a growing hunger force you to commit.
  • Dates are minigames. Keep them charmed before suspicion peaks.
